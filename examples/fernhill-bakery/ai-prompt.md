## Brand
Name: Fernhill Bakery
Tagline: Bread worth walking for
Personality: welcoming, craft-minded, honest
Voice: warm, unfussy
Audience: neighbors who walk to their local bakery on weekend mornings

## Voice guidance
Write the way a friendly baker talks across the counter: short sentences, plain words, no marketing gloss. Name the actual loaf or pastry instead of saying "our products". Humor is fine when it's gentle; never hard-sell.

## Colors
Hero: Pine #2F5D50
Accent: Butter #F2D58A, Apricot #F4A98C, Mist #DDEBE4
Light neutral: Flour #FAF7F0
Dark neutral: Espresso #2B1D14
Approved text/background pairs: Espresso on Flour (15.2:1), Flour on Pine (7.0:1), Espresso on Butter (11.4:1)

## Typography
Headings: Fraunces
Body: Source Sans 3

## Buttons
Filled, slightly rounded corners. Default: Pine bg / Flour text. Hover: Butter bg / Espresso text.

## Logo
Primary: wordmark with a small wheat-ear mark. Minimum width 120px. Keep clear space equal to the height of the "F" on all sides.
